FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

What file formats does Identra AI support?

Identra AI supports PDF documents, Microsoft Word files (.docx), PNG/JPG images, marksheets, and digital certificate credentials.

How does the Knowledge Graph build node connections?

Our cognitive engine parses extracted text and maps relationships between your verified skills, tools, educational degrees, and project experiences automatically.

Is my document data private and secure?

Yes. All uploaded files are encrypted with enterprise-grade standards and processed within your isolated user identity workspace.

Can I export my resume and knowledge graph data?

Absolutely! You can export customized ATS-friendly PDFs, JSON node network structures, and text summaries anytime with one click.
